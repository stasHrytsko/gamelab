# Site home — mockup in the "quiet paper" style

Mockup of the Stazzi home page after the reference board
`Screenshot_20261003_171120_ChatGPT.jpg` (Puzzle Games UI Kit, "Quiet Paper
Puzzle", from the Drive folder "My site").

Not PuzzleKit: the site has its own look, like the hub did before. Taken from
the reference board:

- **Type:** serif, Lora (has Cyrillic for the Ukrainian version). Bold headings,
  small widely tracked caps for captions, ghost links are underlined text.
- **Colour (read from small print, approximate):** Paper `#ede6d8`,
  Blue `#86a0c2`, Terracotta `#ce7a68`, Ochre `#d7a64a`, Sage `#8da68f`,
  Charcoal `#3b3b3b`.
- **Surface:** paper grain over the whole page, cards with slightly rough
  (deckled) edges and soft short shadows, small radius (10–14 px), torn-paper
  footer edge, paper triangles / mountains / sun / dried sprig as decoration.
- **Layout (agreed):** hero with text on the left and the portrait on the
  right; Games = newest big card + two small + "More"; Blog = same; Contacts.

Files: `index.html` (self-contained, fonts in `assets/fonts/`), `render.mjs`
(rebuilds `preview-desktop.png` 1440 px and `preview-mobile.png` 390 px).
Game covers here are paper-collage placeholders; real art comes later.
